## Relation to DXKB

The Bacterial and Viral Bioinformatics Resource Center ([BV-BRC](https://bv-brc.org)) is an information system designed to support research on bacterial and viral infectious diseases.

BV-BRC is a sister-site to the Disease X Knowledge Base ([DXKB](https://dxkb.org)), most of the backend services are shared between the two sites.

## BV-BRC Documentation

For more information on [BV-BRC](https://bv-brc.org), please refer to the [BV-BRC Documentation](https://bv-brc.org/docs).
