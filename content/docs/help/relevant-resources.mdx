This page contains resources that are relevant to the DXKB project.

## BV-BRC

The [BV-BRC](https://www.bv-brc.org/) is a resource for the study of bacterial virulence.
This is the sister-site to DXKB, and the two sites are closely integrated in terms of backend services.

## CEPI

The [Coalition for Epidemic Preparedness Innovations (CEPI)](https://www.cepi.org/) is a global public-private partnership working to prevent and prepare for epidemic threats.
The DXKB project is funded directly by CEPI.

## University of Chicago

The [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu/) is a private research university located in Chicago, Illinois.
The DXKB project is hosted by the University of Chicago Consortium for Advanced Science and Engineering (CASE).

## University of Chicago Consortium for Advanced Science and Engineering

Through joint appointments, [University of Chicago Consortium for Advanced Science and Engineering (CASE)](https://researchdevelopment.uchicago.edu/uchicago-case/) brings new research, training and mentoring opportunities to scientists at the National Laboratories in engineering, public policy and the computational, biological, physical, and social sciences.

## Argonne National Laboratory

The [Argonne National Laboratory](https://www.anl.gov/) is a national laboratory managed by the University of Chicago for the U.S. Department of Energy.
The DXKB project is a collaboration between the University of Chicago, Argonne National Laboratory, and the Coalition for Epidemic Preparedness Innovations (CEPI).
